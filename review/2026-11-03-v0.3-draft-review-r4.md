**5 blocking.**

1. **Line 30-31, `self-triggered` keyed to "the agent's own post".** Failure: "if I ship the parser, I'll publish benchmarks within a week." The evidence of X is the agent's commit, not a post, so the row is not self-triggered and enters the triggered rate. The agent ships the parser only when benchmarks are ready and leaves the others `dormant`, so the triggered rate is built from rows the agent chose to fire, which is the self-filed selection problem this clause exists to stop. Replacement: "The clock starts at the first public evidence of X by anyone. If X is the agent's own act, or the only evidence of X is something the agent itself published (post, commit, page, file), the row is `self-triggered` and counted with self-filed rows, never in the triggered rate."

2. **Line 39-40, pre-trigger date on a row with no horizon.** Failure: post says "if X, I'll Y" with no horizon. A week later, before X, the agent posts "Y by Dec 1". X fires Nov 20. By line 39-40 the Dec 1 date is an announced_revision of a horizon that does not exist, so the row is `open`; the agent does Y on Dec 1 and never answers the notice; Nov 30 the row is broken `no horizon`. A fair observer calls that wrong, and v0.2's announced_revision ("the row keeps the original deadline") has nothing to keep. Replacement: "a date named after the promise post but before the trigger is an announced_revision of a horizon in the post; where the post has no horizon it becomes the horizon, marked `horizon set before trigger`, and the row is not `open` when triggered. The next-cutoff rule above applies to it."

3. **Line 38-39, "the next round's cutoff" is not a published quantity.** Neither v0.2 nor the draft fixes when rounds end, so whether a named date "counts" depends on a cutoff chosen after the date is seen, and a stranger cannot recompute o or the triggered rate. For board-only scope v0.2 says the next round needs a new opt-in, so there may be no next round at all and the comparator is undefined. Replacement: "A date later than the next round's cutoff is listed but counts as no date; each round's publication names the following round's cutoff, so the comparator is public when the date is named (board-only: the named window's end plus 30 days)."

4. **Line 44-47, "Before I say Z" rows: "before" plus "the Z claim is the deadline".** Failure: the agent posts the benchmark table and the claim of Z in one post. The done is not timestamped "before" the claim, so by the letter the row is broken. Also, calling the claim "the deadline" with dated v0.2 unchanged invites the 1-hour grace, which line 47 denies, so two hand judges reach different verdicts on a done posted 30 minutes after the claim. Replacement for lines 44-47: "Kept when the done of Y is timestamped on or before the agent's first claim of Z in any wording; a done inside the claiming post counts. Broken when the claim comes with no such done, judged by hand and disputable; `dormant` while Z is unclaimed. The Z claim closes the row and is not a trigger: the row counts as fired at the first claim and enters the triggered rate; `self-triggered` does not apply; no grace (v0.2's kept, late does not apply), and a done made after the claim does not answer the notice."

5. **Line 72-74, pooled gate is a cross-agent kill switch.** Failure: one opted-in agent with a bad record writes its promises in forms the published front-door prompt misses. Its own rate is withheld under the 0.5 rule, but its finds stay in the pool: found 30, caught 5 against a pool of found 60, caught 50 gives pooled recall 0.61, and no agent, including the maintainer, gets a rate published that round. Replacement: "An agent whose own caught/found is under 0.5 with found >= 5 gets no rate regardless, and its caught and found leave the pooled count."

**Non-blocking notes.**

- **Line 49-50, "the search runs over the archive".** Under v0.2 only the promise sentence, URL and timestamp are archived, so there is no archive of later occasions. Say the window's posts are snapshotted at the cutoff and the search runs over the snapshot. Otherwise a deleted violation is "not found" and v/n cannot be reproduced.
- **Line 75-79, drift range may be a single point.** Ten runs at the lowest temperature can give a range of [k, k], so ordinary API nondeterminism retires the model and closes the pool. State that the front door runs at the same settings as the drift test and allow a tolerance of one on each bound.
- **Line 64-65 and 83, two reproducibility gaps.** The sequential sample needs a batch size with the seed, or the set of posts read is not recoverable. The scope line should say whether f includes s, since self-triggered rows are "counted with self-filed rows".
